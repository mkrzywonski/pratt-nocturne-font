FONTLOG for Pratt Nocturne

Pratt Nocturne is an original drawn interpretation of unusual handwriting,
with suspended heads, falling vowels, boxy curves and projecting serifs.
It is a display family with four styles and extended Latin support.
The fonts are licensed under SIL OFL 1.1, with no Reserved Font Names.

2.001 — First packaged public release
- Regular, Bold, Oblique and Bold Oblique.
- 426 encoded characters and 475 glyphs in each style.
- Full Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, Romanian comma-below letters,
  publishing symbols, common fractions and combining accents.
- OpenType kerning, contextual and stylistic alternates, small figures,
  common fraction substitutions and mark positioning.
- Lowercase d has mirrored counterparts to b's top and foot serifs;
  d-caron and barred d inherit the change.
- Includes TTF, OTF and WOFF2 distributions.

2.000 — Initial complete Latin family
- Expanded repertoire and restrained eight-degree oblique companions.
- Added accent attachment, style linking and publishing characters.

Earlier development
- Established the core alphabet and the signature a, e, D, r, R and 9.
- Refined shared stem, bowl and numeral constructions.
