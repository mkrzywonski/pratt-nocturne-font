Pratt Nocturne is an eccentric handwriting-inspired display family with
suspended heads, descending vowels, boxy curves, and unexpected serifs.

**Four styles:** Regular, Bold, Oblique, and Bold Oblique.
**Coverage:** 426 characters and 488 glyphs per style.
**License:** SIL Open Font License 1.1, with no Reserved Font Names.

### Which download?

- **Desktop-TTF.zip** — recommended for most users; all four styles,
  installation notes, license, font history and a short specimen PDF.
- **Desktop-OTF.zip** — the same family in OpenType/CFF format. Install
  TTF or OTF, not both.
- **Webfonts.zip** — eight WOFF2 files, CSS, a working HTML example and
  the font license.
- **DaFont.zip** — the compact directory-submission package.
- **Public-Specimen.pdf** — four pages of book-cover, correspondence,
  poster and family examples.
- **Public-Preview.png** — a standalone preview image.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** — checksums for the supplied downloads.

The automatically generated source archives contain the editable project.
The ready-to-install packages contain no source photographs.

### Version 2.011

Refines K with a shortened floating stem, a penetrating junction and R's
straight descending leg. Simplifies ? to its dark, narrow 7-like stroke
and dot. The new @ uses the tiny T with a continuous enclosing tail,
without the detached slash, in both families.

K-comma-below and the inverted question mark inherit the revised forms.
Readable a and q remain available through ss02 and the separate Readable
family. Both families retain all four styles.

The font files passed OpenType Sanitizer, repertoire and metadata checks,
and HarfBuzz shaping checks. Best used at display sizes with generous
line spacing; no manual small-size hinting is provided.
