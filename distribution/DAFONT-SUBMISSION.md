## Description / author's note

Pratt Nocturne is an eccentric handwriting-inspired display family with
suspended letterforms, falling vowels, boxy curves, and unexpected serifs.
It began with a fascination for the unusual shapes in an old handwritten
letter and grew into an original, carefully drawn typeface.

Four styles—Regular, Bold, Oblique, and Bold Oblique—include 426 characters
per style, extended Latin support, and expressive OpenType alternates.
Best for book covers, posters, poetry, correspondence, and short passages
that need a distinctive voice.

Free for personal and commercial use under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
No Reserved Font Names are declared. The full license is included.

Source files, updates, specimens and issue reporting:
https://github.com/mkrzywonski/pratt-nocturne-font
